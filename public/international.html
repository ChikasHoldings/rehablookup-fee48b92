Frequently Asked Questions

Can international clients get addiction treatment in the United States?

Yes, many U.S. treatment centers accept international clients. Requirements vary by facility, but most require a valid passport, appropriate visa (typically B-2 for medical treatment), proof of payment ability, and medical records. Our placement team helps coordinate all documentation.

What visa do I need for addiction treatment in the USA?

Most international clients enter on a B-2 tourist visa with medical treatment purposes noted. Some may qualify for other visa categories. Treatment duration typically ranges from 30-90 days, though longer programs are available. We provide documentation to support your visa application.

How much does U.S. addiction treatment cost for international clients?

Costs vary significantly based on program type, duration, and amenities. Luxury residential programs range from $30,000-$100,000+ per month, while quality standard programs cost $15,000-$40,000 per month. Our team helps you find options within your budget and explores payment plans where available.

Why choose the United States for addiction treatment?

The U.S. offers some of the world's most advanced addiction treatment programs, including cutting-edge therapies, evidence-based approaches, and specialized programs for executives, celebrities, and complex cases. Many facilities offer complete privacy, luxury amenities, and comprehensive medical care.

Does RehabLookup help with travel and accommodation for family members?

Yes, our international placement service includes coordination of travel logistics, accommodation recommendations for accompanying family members, and guidance on family program participation. Many facilities offer family therapy weeks and visitor-friendly policies.
