About Meth Rehab

Methamphetamine programs use contingency management, the Matrix Model (16-week intensive outpatient), and structured exercise. No FDA-approved MAT exists; recent trials of mirtazapine and naltrexone+bupropion show modest signal. Recovery from cognitive deficits can take 6–12 months of sustained abstinence.

Insurance Accepted in Virginia

Virginia expanded Medicaid under the ACA, broadening coverage for adults up to 138% of the federal poverty line. Under the federal Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act, most plans must cover substance-use treatment at parity with medical/surgical benefits.
