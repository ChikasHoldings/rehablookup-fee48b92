Types of Addiction Treatment Programs

Understanding the different levels of care helps you find the right treatment intensity for your unique situation and recovery goals.

Finding the Right Level of Care

Addiction treatment isn't one-size-fits-all. The right program depends on several factors including the severity of addiction, co-occurring mental health conditions, previous treatment history, and personal circumstances like work and family obligations.

Treatment programs are organized into different levels of care, from intensive medical detox to flexible outpatient services. Many people progress through multiple levels as they move through their recovery journey.

Treatment Program Categories

Medical Detoxification

Medically supervised withdrawal management for those with physical dependence. Typically lasts 3-10 days and provides 24/7 medical monitoring to ensure safety during withdrawal.

Learn about detox programs →

Inpatient / Residential

Immersive treatment with 24-hour care in a structured environment. Programs typically last 30-90 days and include therapy, education, and peer support away from triggers.

Explore residential treatment →

Partial Hospitalization (PHP)

Intensive day treatment with 5-7 hours of programming daily. Clients return home or to sober living at night while receiving near-residential level care.

Learn about PHP programs →

Intensive Outpatient (IOP)

Flexible treatment option with 3-4 hours of programming, 3-5 days per week. Allows clients to maintain work and family responsibilities while in active treatment.

Explore outpatient options →

Dual Diagnosis Treatment

Specialized programs treating addiction alongside co-occurring mental health conditions like depression, anxiety, PTSD, or bipolar disorder.

Find dual diagnosis care →

Medication-Assisted Treatment

Combines FDA-approved medications (like Suboxone, Vivitrol, or methadone) with counseling and behavioral therapies for opioid and alcohol addiction.

Learn about MAT →

ASAM Levels of Care

4

Medically Managed Intensive Inpatient

Hospital-level care for severe withdrawal or medical complications requiring 24-hour nursing and physician care.

3

Residential / Inpatient Treatment

24-hour structured care with clinical services. Includes high-intensity residential and clinically managed programs.

2

Intensive Outpatient / Partial Hospitalization

Structured outpatient services 9+ hours per week. PHP typically 20+ hours; IOP typically 9-19 hours weekly.

1

Outpatient Treatment

Less than 9 hours of services per week. Includes individual counseling, group therapy, and recovery support.
