Frequently Asked Questions

What is the RehabLookup Concierge Placement Service?

Our concierge service provides personalized help finding the right addiction treatment center. Our placement specialists assess your unique needs, verify your insurance benefits, connect you with appropriate facilities, and guide you through the entire admission process.

How much does the concierge placement service cost?

There is a one-time service fee of $299 to begin working with our placement team. This covers your dedicated specialist, insurance verification, facility placement, and ongoing support throughout your treatment journey. There are no hidden fees or commissions.

How long does it take to get placed at a treatment center?

Most clients receive their first facility options within 24-48 hours of completing the intake form. For urgent situations, we can often expedite the process and have options ready the same day. Our team works around the clock to ensure timely placement.

What if I don't have insurance?

We work with clients regardless of insurance status. Our network includes facilities that offer sliding scale fees, payment plans, scholarship programs, and state-funded options. We'll help you explore all available financial assistance options.
