Google Business Profile for Rehab Centers: The Complete Optimization Guide

Your Google Business Profile is the #1 factor in local search visibility. 46% of all Google searches have local intent — here's how to make sure families find your facility first.

Why Your Google Business Profile Is Your Most Valuable Digital Asset

For treatment centers, the Google Business Profile (GBP) is more valuable than your website for local patient acquisition. When someone searches 'rehab near me' or 'drug treatment center [city],' Google shows the Map Pack — three local businesses pulled directly from GBP data. Facilities in the Map Pack receive 5x more clicks than organic results below. Yet most treatment centers have incomplete, unoptimized profiles that cost them dozens of admissions every month.

  • 46% of all Google searches have local intent — these are your highest-intent prospects
  • The Map Pack appears in 93% of local searches and gets 44% of all clicks
  • Facilities with complete GBP profiles get 7x more clicks than incomplete ones
  • GBP reviews are the #1 trust signal for families choosing a treatment center
  • Google Business Profile is 100% free — it's the highest-ROI channel available

Complete Profile Setup: Every Field Matters

Google rewards completeness. A fully optimized profile tells Google you're a legitimate, active business worthy of showing to searchers. Every empty field is a missed opportunity. Start with your primary category (Addiction Treatment Center or Substance Abuse Treatment Center), then add secondary categories like 'Drug Rehabilitation Center' and 'Detox Center.' Your business description should be 750 characters and include your key services, treatment approaches, and location naturally.

  • Set primary category to 'Addiction Treatment Center' or 'Substance Abuse Treatment Center'
  • Add all relevant secondary categories: Detox Center, Mental Health Clinic, Counseling Center
  • Write a compelling 750-character business description with natural keyword placement
  • Add your website, phone number, address, and hours (24/7 for residential facilities)
  • Enable messaging and appointment booking features for faster inquiry response

Photos and Visual Content That Build Trust

Listings with photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more click-throughs to websites. Families choosing a treatment center want to see where their loved one will be staying. Professional, warm facility photos dramatically reduce the anxiety families feel about the treatment process. Update your photos quarterly to signal to Google that your profile is actively maintained.

  • Upload 25+ high-quality photos: exterior, common areas, rooms, therapy spaces, dining areas
  • Add a professional logo and cover photo that reflects your brand
  • Include photos of your team (with consent) to build personal connection
  • Upload a virtual tour if available — these significantly increase engagement
  • Post new photos monthly to maintain freshness signals for Google's algorithm

Reviews: The Growth Engine for Treatment Centers

Google reviews are the single most influential factor in a family's decision to call your facility. Treatment centers with 50+ reviews and a 4.5+ star rating receive 3x more inquiries than those with fewer than 10 reviews. Implement a systematic review collection process: ask satisfied alumni and families at discharge, send a follow-up email 30 days post-treatment, and always respond to every review — positive and negative — within 24 hours.

  • Aim for 50+ genuine reviews with a 4.5+ star average rating
  • Implement a systematic review request at discharge and 30-day follow-up
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours — Google rewards engagement
  • Never buy fake reviews — Google penalizes this and it destroys trust
  • Address negative reviews professionally and offer to resolve issues offline

Google Posts: Stay Visible Between Searches

Google Posts are free micro-content published directly on your GBP. They appear in your Knowledge Panel and keep your profile active. Post weekly updates about your programs, community events, success milestones, and educational content about addiction and recovery. Each post should include a call-to-action button linking to your admissions page or phone number.

  • Publish at least one Google Post per week to maintain profile freshness
  • Use 'Call Now' or 'Learn More' CTAs on every post for direct action
  • Share educational content: signs of addiction, treatment options, recovery tips
  • Announce new programs, certifications, or staff additions
  • Include relevant images with each post — posts with images get 10x more engagement
