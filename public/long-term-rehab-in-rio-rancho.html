About Long-Term Rehab

Long-term residential (90+ days) suits patients with repeated relapse, polysubstance use, unstable housing, or significant trauma histories. Programs typically pair extended clinical care with vocational support and gradual community re-integration. Therapeutic communities (TCs) are a long-established model.

Insurance Accepted in New mexico

New mexico expanded Medicaid under the ACA, broadening coverage for adults up to 138% of the federal poverty line. Under the federal Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act, most plans must cover substance-use treatment at parity with medical/surgical benefits.
