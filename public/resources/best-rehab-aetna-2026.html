Best Rehab Centers That Accept Aetna Insurance in 2026

By RehabLookup Editorial 2026-04-07 10 min read min read
How to maximize your Aetna benefits for addiction treatment, which facilities are in-network, and what your actual out-of-pocket costs will be.

Aetna insures over 34 million Americans and maintains one of the broader behavioral health networks among commercial insurers. If you hold an Aetna plan — whether through your employer, the ACA marketplace, or Medicare Advantage — you have coverage for addiction treatment. The question is understanding exactly what your specific plan covers and how to get the most from your benefits without unexpected costs.

What Aetna covers for substance abuse treatment

Aetna follows Mental Health Parity requirements, which means substance use disorder treatment must be covered at the same level as medical and surgical benefits. Standard covered services include medically supervised detoxification, inpatient residential treatment (typically 28 to 30 days initially), partial hospitalization programs, intensive outpatient programs (usually 3 to 5 days per week), individual and group therapy, and medication-assisted treatment.

Aetna uses a utilization management process where treatment is authorized in increments. The initial authorization for residential is typically 7 to 14 days, with extensions based on clinical need. This does not mean your treatment is limited to that timeframe — it means the facility needs to demonstrate medical necessity for continued stay.

Aetna plan types and how they affect rehab coverage

  • Aetna PPO: Most flexibility. You can use out-of-network providers with higher cost-sharing (typically 60-70% coverage vs. 80-90% in-network)
  • Aetna HMO: Must use in-network providers and get referrals. Lower premiums but less choice in treatment facilities
  • Aetna EPO: In-network only like an HMO, but no referral needed. Good balance if your preferred facility is in-network
  • Aetna Medicare Advantage: Covers addiction treatment for adults 65+. Includes Part D coverage for MAT medications
  • Aetna Student Health: Often covers residential treatment but may have annual day limits. Check with your university health center

How to verify your Aetna rehab benefits

Call (800) 872-3862 (Aetna Member Services) and have your member ID ready. Ask specifically about behavioral health and substance use disorder benefits. Request your deductible, copay or coinsurance for each level of care, out-of-pocket maximum, and whether prior authorization is required. Write everything down and get a reference number for the call.

Top Aetna in-network facilities by state

Aetna maintains strong in-network relationships with many nationally recognized treatment programs. In Florida, major in-network facilities include programs in Palm Beach, Fort Lauderdale, and Tampa. In California, Aetna covers programs in Los Angeles, Orange County, and the San Francisco Bay Area. Texas, Arizona, and Colorado also have robust Aetna networks with multiple residential options.

Aetna also contracts with national organizations like Hazelden Betty Ford Foundation, American Addiction Centers, and many regional nonprofits, giving members access to quality treatment across the country.

What to do if Aetna denies coverage

  • Request the denial in writing with the specific reason and plan provision cited
  • Ask your treatment team to conduct a peer-to-peer review with Aetna medical director
  • File a formal internal appeal within 180 days of the denial
  • If the internal appeal is denied, file an external review through your state insurance department
  • Contact your state attorney general if you believe Aetna is violating parity law

Frequently asked questions

Does Aetna cover rehab for my adult child on my plan?

Yes. Under the ACA, dependents can remain on a parent plan until age 26. Substance use treatment is covered the same as for the primary policyholder. However, the dependent must consent to treatment, and privacy regulations (42 CFR Part 2) prevent the insurer from disclosing treatment details to the policyholder without consent.

How much will I pay out of pocket with Aetna?

Typical out-of-pocket costs for in-network residential treatment with Aetna range from $1,000 to $5,000 after deductible, depending on your plan. Many plans have an out-of-pocket maximum of $8,000 to $16,000 for the year. Once you hit that maximum, Aetna covers 100% of remaining costs.
