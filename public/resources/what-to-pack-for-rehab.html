What to Pack for Rehab: Complete Packing List and Guidelines

By RehabLookup Editorial Team 2026-02-07 7 min read min read
Preparing to enter rehab? This comprehensive packing guide covers what to bring, what's prohibited, and tips for a comfortable stay.

Packing for rehab can feel overwhelming. This guide helps you prepare with confidence, covering what to bring, what to leave behind, and tips for a smoother transition.

Clothing Essentials

  • 7-10 days of casual, comfortable clothing
  • Workout clothes for fitness activities
  • Pajamas and sleepwear
  • Comfortable walking shoes and sandals
  • Jacket or sweater (facilities are often air-conditioned)
Avoid clothing with drug/alcohol references. Most facilities have dress codes.

Personal Care Items

  • Shampoo, conditioner, body wash
  • Toothbrush and toothpaste
  • Deodorant (non-aerosol)
  • Hairbrush or comb
  • Prescription glasses or contacts

Important Documents

  • Photo ID
  • Insurance cards
  • List of current medications
  • Emergency contact information
  • Prescription bottles for verification

Items Typically Prohibited

  • Cell phones (often restricted)
  • Laptops and tablets
  • Over-the-counter medications
  • Aerosol products
  • Mouthwash containing alcohol

Comfort Items

  • Photos of loved ones
  • Journal or notebook
  • Books (subject to approval)
  • Stamps and envelopes for letters
