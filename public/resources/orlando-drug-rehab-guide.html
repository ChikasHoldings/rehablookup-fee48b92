Orlando and Central Florida offer a supportive environment for addiction recovery, with a growing network of treatment facilities and a strong recovery community. Away from the tourist areas, Orlando provides serious, professional treatment options for both local residents and those traveling for care.

Central Florida Treatment Landscape

Orange County and surrounding areas host over 50 licensed treatment facilities, ranging from hospital-based medical programs to residential treatment centers. Orlando's mild climate and family-friendly atmosphere support long-term recovery.

Available Treatments

  • Medical Detox - Supervised withdrawal management
  • Residential Programs - Immersive 30-90 day treatment
  • Outpatient Services - Flexible scheduling for work/family
  • Young Adult Programs - Age-specific treatment approaches
  • Family Programs - Comprehensive family involvement

Insurance and Payment

Orlando facilities accept most major insurance including BCBS Florida, Aetna, Cigna, United Healthcare, and Florida Medicaid. Florida funds treatment through Managing Entities for uninsured residents.

Orlando Area Locations

  • Downtown Orlando - Urban outpatient services
  • Winter Park - Upscale residential programs
  • Kissimmee - Family-focused treatment
  • Lake Mary - Suburban residential options
  • Altamonte Springs - Community-based care
Central Florida's recovery community hosts numerous meetings, sober events, and alumni networks. Orlando is an ideal location for long-term recovery support.
