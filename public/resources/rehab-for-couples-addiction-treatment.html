Rehab for Couples: How Partners Can Recover Together

By RehabLookup Editorial Team April 4, 2026 9 min read min read
Explore couples rehab programs that allow partners to recover together. Learn about program types, benefits, and how to find treatment.

When both partners struggle with addiction, treating one while the other continues using rarely works. Couples rehab programs address this by treating both partners simultaneously.

What is couples rehab?

Couples rehab is specialized addiction treatment where both partners participate in recovery together, combining individual therapy with joint couples therapy, group sessions, and relationship skill-building.

Types of couples treatment programs

  • Inpatient couples rehab: both partners live at the facility (30 to 90 days)
  • Outpatient couples programs: attend sessions while living at home
  • Intensive outpatient (IOP): structured programming several days per week
  • Behavioral couples therapy (BCT): evidence-based approach

Benefits of treating addiction together

  • Removes the enabling dynamic
  • Both partners develop healthy communication skills
  • Shared accountability and mutual support
  • Addresses relationship trauma and codependency
  • Higher retention rates when both partners are motivated

When couples rehab may not be appropriate

Programs screen for domestic violence. If there is active abuse, significant power imbalance, or one partner is not committed, individual treatment may be more appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

How much does couples rehab cost?

Costs range from $10,000 to $60,000 per couple for 30-day inpatient. Outpatient programs are $3,000 to $10,000.

Do couples share a room?

Policies vary by facility. Some offer shared rooms for married couples while others house partners separately.
