San Antonio, the seventh-largest city in the United States, offers comprehensive addiction treatment services for its diverse population. With a strong military presence, Hispanic community, and growing healthcare infrastructure, the Alamo City provides culturally responsive treatment options. This guide covers what you need to know about finding rehab in San Antonio.

San Antonio Treatment Overview

San Antonio's healthcare sector has expanded significantly, including addiction treatment services. Major systems include University Health, Methodist Healthcare, Baptist Health, and the South Texas VA Healthcare System. The city offers bilingual treatment programs and services designed for veterans and military families.

Types of Treatment in San Antonio

  • Medical Detoxification - Hospital-based and standalone detox
  • Residential Treatment - 30-90 day programs throughout Bexar County
  • Veterans Programs - VA and community programs for military families
  • Partial Hospitalization (PHP) - Day treatment options
  • Intensive Outpatient (IOP) - Evening and weekend programs
  • Faith-Based Programs - Christian and spiritually-oriented treatment

Treatment by San Antonio Area

Downtown & South Side

Central San Antonio hosts major hospital systems and community health centers. University Health provides comprehensive services including addiction treatment. Numerous outpatient programs serve the downtown area and surrounding neighborhoods.

North Side

North San Antonio and surrounding areas like Stone Oak and Alamo Heights have private treatment facilities and outpatient programs. This area tends to offer more executive and private-pay options.

Military Corridor

The areas near Joint Base San Antonio (Fort Sam Houston, Lackland, Randolph) have specialized programs for active military and veterans. The San Antonio VA and TRICARE-accepting facilities provide comprehensive addiction services.

Insurance & Payment Options

Texas requires insurance parity for substance use treatment. TRICARE covers addiction treatment for military families. Texas Medicaid, Medicare, and major private insurers provide coverage. San Antonio also has programs accepting self-pay and offering sliding scale fees.
