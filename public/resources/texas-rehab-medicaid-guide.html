Texas Rehab: Addiction Treatment Options and Medicaid Coverage

By RehabLookup Editorial Team February 7, 2026 10 min read min read
Navigate addiction treatment options in Texas, including Medicaid coverage, major treatment centers, and state-specific resources.

Texas, with its vast geography and diverse population, presents unique challenges and opportunities for those seeking addiction treatment. From major metropolitan areas like Houston and Dallas to rural communities, understanding your options is essential for finding appropriate care.

Texas Addiction Treatment Overview

Texas has over 800 licensed substance abuse treatment facilities offering various levels of care. The Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC) oversees licensing and regulation. Major treatment hubs are concentrated in Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, San Antonio, and Austin.

Medicaid Coverage in Texas

Texas has not expanded Medicaid under the Affordable Care Act, which limits coverage for many low-income adults. However, Texas Medicaid does cover some addiction treatment services for those who qualify, including pregnant women, children, elderly individuals, and people with disabilities.

For those who do not qualify for Medicaid, Texas offers state-funded treatment through the HHSC. Contact your local mental health authority (LMHA) to access these services.

Private Insurance in Texas

Texas law requires most health plans to cover treatment for chemical dependency. Large group plans must cover at least 28 days of inpatient treatment and 60 days of outpatient treatment per year. However, coverage varies significantly, so always verify your specific benefits.

Regional Treatment Options

Houston: The state's largest city has numerous treatment options, from major hospital-based programs to specialized facilities. Dallas-Fort Worth: Strong network of treatment centers with diverse approaches. San Antonio: Growing treatment community with bilingual options. Austin: Known for innovative, progressive treatment approaches. Rural Texas: Fewer options but state-funded programs help fill gaps.

Texas-Specific Resources

The Texas 211 helpline (dial 211) connects callers with local treatment resources. The state's peer support certification program has expanded recovery support services across Texas. Many communities offer drug courts as alternatives to incarceration.

Challenges in Texas Treatment

Texas faces unique challenges including geographic barriers in rural areas, limited Medicaid coverage, and high uninsured rates. However, the state has invested in expanding treatment capacity and improving access, particularly for opioid use disorder.
