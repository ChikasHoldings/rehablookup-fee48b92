Drug Rehab in Austin: Guide to Addiction Treatment in Central Texas

By RehabLookup Editorial Team February 2026 10 min read min read
Find drug and alcohol rehab centers in Austin, Texas. Compare treatment programs, verify insurance coverage, and discover recovery resources in the Texas capital.

Austin, Texas's capital and one of the fastest-growing cities in America, offers diverse addiction treatment options for its growing population. From innovative programs serving the tech workforce to community-based services, Austin provides comprehensive care for substance use disorders. This guide covers addiction treatment resources in Central Texas.

Austin's Treatment Environment

Austin's progressive culture extends to its approach to addiction treatment. The city embraces evidence-based practices, harm reduction, and innovative recovery models. Major health systems including Ascension Seton, St. David's, and Dell Medical School at UT Austin provide comprehensive addiction services.

Types of Treatment in Austin

  • Medical Detoxification - Hospital-affiliated and standalone programs
  • Residential Treatment - 30-90 day programs in Austin and surrounding areas
  • Partial Hospitalization (PHP) - Day treatment at major medical centers
  • Intensive Outpatient (IOP) - Flexible programs for working professionals
  • Young Adult Programs - Specialized treatment for 18-30 year olds
  • Executive Treatment - Programs designed for professionals

Treatment by Austin Area

Central Austin

Downtown and central Austin host numerous outpatient programs and connections to hospital-based services. Dell Seton Medical Center and other facilities provide acute care. The area also has strong recovery community support.

North Austin & Round Rock

North Austin and Williamson County have expanded treatment options as the area has grown. Several residential facilities operate in Cedar Park, Round Rock, and Georgetown, offering quieter treatment environments.

South Austin & San Marcos

South Austin and Hays County provide treatment options serving diverse populations. San Marcos, home to Texas State University, has programs addressing young adult addiction.

Texas Insurance Coverage

Texas mental health parity laws require insurers to cover substance abuse treatment. Texas Medicaid and CHIP cover addiction services for eligible residents. Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas, United Healthcare, Aetna, and other major insurers provide substance abuse benefits.
