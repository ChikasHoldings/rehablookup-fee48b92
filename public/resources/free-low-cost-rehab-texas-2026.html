Free and Low-Cost Rehab Centers in Texas: Complete 2026 List

By RehabLookup Editorial 2026-04-07 12 min read min read
Every free and affordable addiction treatment option in Texas, from state-funded programs to nonprofit residential centers and Medicaid-covered facilities.

Texas faces one of the highest rates of substance use disorder in the country, and while the state has not expanded Medicaid under the ACA, there are still significant free and low-cost treatment options available. The Texas Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC) funds a network of community-based programs, and federal grants supplement local options. Here is every avenue available for affordable treatment in Texas.

Texas state-funded treatment programs

HHSC funds substance abuse treatment through Local Mental Health Authorities (LMHAs) and contracted providers across all 254 Texas counties. These programs serve uninsured and underinsured Texans at no cost or on a sliding-scale basis. Services include outpatient counseling, intensive outpatient programs, residential treatment, and detoxification.

To access state-funded treatment, call the HHSC helpline at (877) 541-7905 or contact your local LMHA. Wait times for residential treatment can range from a few days to several weeks depending on the region. Urban areas like Houston, Dallas, and San Antonio tend to have more capacity but also higher demand.

Region-by-region guide

Houston and Southeast Texas

The Harris Center for Mental Health and IDD serves as the LMHA for Harris County and provides free assessments and referrals. The Council on Recovery, Santa Maria Hostel, and Memorial Hermann offer free or low-cost programs. The Houston Recovery Center provides free sobering services and crisis intervention 24/7.

Dallas-Fort Worth

North Texas Behavioral Health Authority coordinates free treatment referrals for Dallas County. Nexus Recovery Center provides free residential treatment for women. Homeward Bound operates free transitional housing with treatment services. The Salvation Army has free residential programs in both Dallas and Fort Worth.

San Antonio and Central Texas

The Center for Health Care Services is San Antonio's LMHA and provides free substance abuse assessments. Alpha Home offers free residential treatment for women. The Salvation Army Adult Rehabilitation Center provides free long-term residential treatment. Austin's Integral Care offers sliding-scale outpatient services.

Rio Grande Valley and West Texas

Tropical Texas Behavioral Health covers the Rio Grande Valley with bilingual services. Emergence Health Network serves the El Paso area. These regions have fewer facilities but shorter wait times than major metro areas. Telehealth options have expanded significantly since 2024.

Texas Medicaid and CHIP coverage

While Texas has not expanded Medicaid to all low-income adults, traditional Medicaid covers addiction treatment for pregnant women, parents of dependent children meeting income requirements, SSI recipients, and children under CHIP. If you qualify, treatment is covered with minimal or no copay.

Nonprofit and faith-based options

  • Salvation Army Adult Rehabilitation Centers — Free residential in Houston, Dallas, San Antonio, Austin, Fort Worth, El Paso, and Corpus Christi
  • Teen Challenge Texas — Free faith-based residential programs in multiple cities, 12 to 18 month programs
  • Victory Outreach — Free faith-based residential recovery homes across Texas
  • Palmer Drug Abuse Program — Free outpatient treatment for youth and young adults in Houston
  • Santa Maria Hostel — Free residential for women and children in Houston
Even without Medicaid expansion, Texas residents have access to free treatment through state-funded, federal, and nonprofit programs. Call (877) 541-7905 or search RehabLookup to find options near you.

How to get started today

  • Call SAMHSA National Helpline: 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7)
  • Call Texas HHSC: (877) 541-7905 for state-funded program referrals
  • Call 211 Texas for local resource referrals
  • Visit findtreatment.gov and filter by Texas and free/sliding-scale
  • Search RehabLookup for Texas treatment centers filtered by payment options
  • If you need emergency detox, go to any hospital emergency room
