Understanding Texas Addiction Treatment Laws

Texas regulates addiction treatment facilities through the Health and Human Services Commission (HHSC). The state has invested significantly in expanding treatment access through the Texas Targeted Opioid Response (TTOR) program.

Key Texas Treatment Regulations

  • Facilities licensed by HHSC under Texas Administrative Code Title 26
  • Patient Bill of Rights protects individuals in treatment
  • Good Samaritan Law provides immunity for reporting overdoses
  • Marchman Act allows court-ordered treatment for substance use

Insurance Coverage Options in Texas

Texas Medicaid Coverage

Texas Medicaid covers medically necessary substance abuse treatment services including assessment, detoxification, and rehabilitation. Managed Care Organizations (MCOs) coordinate benefits for most recipients.

Private Insurance in Texas

Texas follows federal parity requirements. Major insurers must provide substance use disorder coverage comparable to medical/surgical benefits.

  • Blue Cross Blue Shield of Texas - largest state provider
  • UnitedHealthcare Texas - comprehensive network
  • Aetna Texas - behavioral health specialty
  • Cigna - extensive outpatient coverage

Treatment Resources by Region

Houston Metro Area

Houston offers extensive treatment options from luxury residential to community-based outpatient programs. Harris County provides crisis services through The Harris Center for Mental Health and IDD.

Dallas-Fort Worth Metroplex

DFW features over 200 licensed treatment facilities. Dallas County's NorthSTAR program provides behavioral health services for uninsured residents.

Austin

Austin combines innovative harm reduction programs with traditional treatment. Integral Care serves as the local mental health and substance abuse authority.

San Antonio

San Antonio's Center for Health Care Services provides a comprehensive continuum of addiction treatment including the state's first recovery-oriented system of care.
