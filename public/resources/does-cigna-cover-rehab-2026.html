Does Cigna Cover Rehab? Everything You Need to Know in 2026

By RehabLookup Editorial 2026-04-07 9 min read min read
A detailed breakdown of Cigna addiction treatment coverage including what is covered, typical costs, how to get authorized, and what to do if Cigna denies your claim.

Cigna Healthcare covers more than 18 million Americans and is required by federal law to cover addiction treatment. But navigating Cigna behavioral health benefits can be confusing — the company uses Evernorth Behavioral Health (formerly Cigna Behavioral Health) as a separate entity to manage substance use disorder claims. Understanding this structure is key to getting your treatment covered smoothly.

What Cigna covers

Cigna covers the full continuum of addiction treatment including medical detoxification, inpatient residential treatment, partial hospitalization, intensive outpatient programs, individual and group counseling, and medication-assisted treatment. All Cigna plans sold after 2010 must comply with the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act, meaning substance use treatment cannot have more restrictive coverage than medical benefits.

Cigna authorization process

Cigna requires prior authorization for residential and inpatient treatment. The treatment facility typically handles this by calling Evernorth Behavioral Health at (800) 274-4621. Initial authorizations are usually for 7 to 14 days, with concurrent reviews every 5 to 7 days. The facility clinical team must demonstrate continued medical necessity for extended stays.

Outpatient and intensive outpatient programs generally do not require prior authorization under most Cigna plans, though the facility should verify this during insurance check.

Typical out-of-pocket costs with Cigna

  • In-network deductible: $500 to $2,500 (individual) depending on plan
  • In-network coinsurance: 10% to 20% after deductible for most plans
  • Out-of-network coinsurance: 30% to 40% after a higher deductible
  • Out-of-pocket maximum: $6,000 to $16,000 (protects you from unlimited costs)
  • Residential treatment: After deductible, typical patient responsibility is $2,000 to $6,000 for a 30-day stay
  • IOP: $20 to $75 per session copay, or coinsurance after deductible

What to do if Cigna denies coverage

Cigna denial rates for residential treatment have been a focus of state regulators. If your claim is denied, request the denial in writing with the specific clinical criteria cited. Ask your treatment provider to schedule a peer-to-peer review with the Cigna medical director. File a formal appeal within the plan timeframe. If the internal appeal fails, you have the right to an independent external review. Many parity violations by Cigna have been identified and overturned through this process.

Frequently asked questions

Does Cigna cover Suboxone and Vivitrol?

Yes. Cigna covers FDA-approved medications for opioid use disorder including buprenorphine (Suboxone), naltrexone (Vivitrol), and methadone maintenance. Copays vary by plan and pharmacy tier. Most plans cover generic buprenorphine at a low copay.

Can I go to rehab out of state with Cigna?

Yes, if the facility is in Cigna network. Cigna maintains a national network, so many facilities across the country accept Cigna plans regardless of where you live. Out-of-network facilities can also be used with PPO plans at higher cost-sharing.
