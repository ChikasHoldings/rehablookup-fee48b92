Frequently Asked Questions

What rehab options are available in Johnson County, KS?

Johnson County offers a range of addiction treatment services including medical detoxification, residential inpatient programs, intensive outpatient programs (IOP), and medication-assisted treatment (MAT). Facilities in and around Olathe and Overland Park, Olathe, Shawnee provide both short-term and long-term recovery options tailored to individual needs.

How much does rehab cost in Johnson County?

Treatment costs in Johnson County vary by program type. Outpatient programs typically range from $2,000–$10,000 for a 90-day course, while residential inpatient treatment averages $5,000–$30,000 for 30 days. Many facilities accept private insurance, Medicaid, and Medicare. Sliding-scale and state-funded options are also available for qualifying residents.

Does insurance cover addiction treatment in Johnson County, Kansas?

Yes. Under the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act, most insurance plans in Kansas must cover substance abuse treatment. This includes employer-sponsored plans, marketplace plans, Medicaid, and Medicare. Contact individual facilities in Johnson County to verify your specific coverage and any out-of-pocket costs.

Are there free or low-cost rehab programs in Johnson County?

Johnson County residents can access free or reduced-cost treatment through state-funded programs, non-profit organizations, and federally qualified health centers. Kansas's substance abuse authority and SAMHSA's helpline (1-800-662-4357) can provide referrals to available no-cost options in the Olathe area.

What types of substances are commonly treated in Johnson County?

Treatment centers in Johnson County address a wide range of substance use disorders including alcohol dependence, opioid and heroin addiction, methamphetamine abuse, cocaine dependency, prescription drug misuse, and polysubstance use. Many programs also treat co-occurring mental health conditions alongside addiction.

How do I find the best rehab center in Johnson County?

When selecting a treatment center in Johnson County, look for accreditation from the Joint Commission or CARF, licensed clinical staff, evidence-based treatment methods, aftercare planning, and positive patient outcomes.
