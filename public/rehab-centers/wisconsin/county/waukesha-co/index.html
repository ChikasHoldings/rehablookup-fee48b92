What rehab options are available in Waukesha County, WI?

Waukesha County offers a range of addiction treatment services including medical detoxification, residential inpatient programs, intensive outpatient programs (IOP), and medication-assisted treatment (MAT). Facilities in and around Waukesha and Waukesha, Brookfield, New Berlin provide both short-term and long-term recovery options tailored to individual needs.

How much does rehab cost in Waukesha County?

Treatment costs in Waukesha County vary by program type. Outpatient programs typically range from $2,000–$10,000 for a 90-day course, while residential inpatient treatment averages $5,000–$30,000 for 30 days. Many facilities accept private insurance, Medicaid, and Medicare. Sliding-scale and state-funded options are also available for qualifying residents.

Does insurance cover addiction treatment in Waukesha County, Wisconsin?

Yes. Under the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act, most insurance plans in Wisconsin must cover substance abuse treatment. This includes employer-sponsored plans, marketplace plans, Medicaid, and Medicare. Contact individual facilities in Waukesha County to verify your specific coverage and any out-of-pocket costs.

Are there free or low-cost rehab programs in Waukesha County?

Waukesha County residents can access free or reduced-cost treatment through state-funded programs, non-profit organizations, and federally qualified health centers. Wisconsin's substance abuse authority and SAMHSA's helpline (1-800-662-4357) can provide referrals to available no-cost options in the Waukesha area.

What types of substances are commonly treated in Waukesha County?

Treatment centers in Waukesha County address a wide range of substance use disorders including alcohol dependence, opioid and heroin addiction, methamphetamine abuse, cocaine dependency, prescription drug misuse, and polysubstance use. Many programs also treat co-occurring mental health conditions alongside addiction.
