About ClearPath Recovery Center

ClearPath Recovery Center is a modern addiction treatment facility dedicated to helping individuals overcome substance use disorders through compassionate, evidence-based care. Our programs are designed to support each client’s unique recovery journey, combining clinical expertise with a calm and structured healing environment. We offer a full continuum of care including medical detox referrals, inpatient treatment, partial hospitalization, intensive outpatient programs, and ongoing aftercare support. Our multidisciplinary team includes licensed therapists, addiction counselors, and medical professionals who work together to address both the physical and psychological aspects of addiction. At ClearPath Recovery Center, we believe recovery is possible with the right support, structure, and individualized care.
