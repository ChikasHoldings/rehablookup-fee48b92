About Pacific Path Recovery Center

Pacific Path Recovery Center is a professionally staffed addiction treatment facility located in Roseville, California, dedicated to helping adults begin and sustain long-term recovery in a safe, structured, and supportive environment. Our center provides individualized treatment plans designed to address substance use disorders while supporting mental, emotional, and behavioral wellness. Our clinical approach combines evidence-based therapies with personalized care, ensuring each client receives the level of support appropriate for their needs. Treatment services are delivered by licensed clinicians and experienced support staff in a calm, private setting that promotes focus, stability, and healing. Pacific Path Recovery Center offers a comprehensive range of programs, including medical detox coordination, residential treatment, and structured outpatient services. We emphasize continuity of care, relapse prevention planning, and life-skills development to support sustainable recovery beyond treatment. The facility features comfortable accommodations, private and semi-private rooms, dedicated therapy spaces, and communal areas designed to foster connection and accountability. Clients participate in individual counseling, group therapy, and recovery-focused educational sessions in an environment that respects dignity and confidentiality. We work closely with families, referral partners, and care coordinators to ensure clear communication and smooth transitions throughout the treatment process. Our admissions team is available to guide individuals and families through intake, insurance verification, and placement coordination. Pacific Path Recovery Center is committed to ethical care, transparency, and client-centered treatment—helping individuals take meaningful steps toward recovery and long-term stability.
