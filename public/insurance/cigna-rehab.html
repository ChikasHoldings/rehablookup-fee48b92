Does Cigna Cover Drug & Alcohol Rehab?

Understanding your Cigna behavioral health benefits for addiction treatment

Cigna Addiction Treatment Coverage Overview

Cigna is one of the largest global health insurance companies, serving over 170 million customers worldwide. Through its behavioral health division, Cigna provides comprehensive coverage for substance abuse treatment at in-network facilities across the United States.

Under federal parity laws, Cigna must cover addiction treatment at the same level as other medical conditions. Most Cigna plans include coverage for:

✓Medical Detox

24/7 medically supervised withdrawal management

✓Inpatient Rehab

Residential treatment programs with comprehensive care

✓Outpatient Programs

PHP, IOP, and individual/group therapy sessions

✓MAT Coverage

Suboxone, Vivitrol, naltrexone, and other medications

Cigna Behavioral Health Network

Cigna maintains an extensive network of addiction treatment providers. Using in-network facilities offers several advantages:

  • Lower out-of-pocket costs (copays and coinsurance)
  • Pre-negotiated rates with providers
  • Streamlined pre-authorization process
  • Direct billing between facility and Cigna

How to Verify Your Cigna Rehab Benefits

Understanding your specific coverage is essential before starting treatment. Here's how to verify your Cigna benefits:

  1. Find your behavioral health number — Look for the behavioral health or mental health services number on your Cigna card, which may differ from the general member services number.
  2. Log into myCigna — Your online account shows detailed benefits information and allows you to search for in-network providers.
  3. Ask key questions — Inquire about your deductible status, copay/coinsurance amounts, out-of-pocket maximum, and any day limits for treatment.
  4. Understand pre-authorization — Ask whether prior approval is required and what information the treatment facility will need to provide.
  5. Get a treatment facility's help — Most rehab centers have admissions staff who verify insurance benefits for free.

Frequently Asked Questions About Cigna Rehab Coverage

Does Cigna cover drug and alcohol rehab?

Yes, Cigna provides coverage for substance abuse treatment through its behavioral health benefits. Most Cigna plans cover medical detox, inpatient rehabilitation, outpatient programs, and medication-assisted treatment. Coverage levels depend on your specific plan and whether you use in-network or out-of-network providers.

What levels of care does Cigna cover for addiction?

Cigna typically covers all clinically appropriate levels of addiction care including medical detoxification, residential treatment (inpatient rehab), partial hospitalization programs (PHP), intensive outpatient programs (IOP), standard outpatient therapy, and medication-assisted treatment (MAT).

How do I verify my Cigna rehab benefits?

Call the behavioral health number on your Cigna card, access your myCigna online account, or contact a treatment facility to verify benefits on your behalf. Ask about deductibles, copays, coinsurance, pre-authorization requirements, and any day limits for inpatient care.

Does Cigna require pre-authorization for rehab?

Most Cigna plans require pre-authorization for inpatient addiction treatment and some outpatient programs. The treatment facility typically handles this process by contacting Cigna to obtain approval before or shortly after admission.

Will Cigna cover residential treatment out of state?

Yes, Cigna has a national provider network and can cover treatment at out-of-state facilities. Coverage depends on whether the facility is in-network and whether your plan includes out-of-network benefits. Always verify coverage before traveling for treatment.

How much does rehab cost with Cigna insurance?

Your out-of-pocket costs depend on your plan's deductible, copay, and coinsurance structure. In-network treatment typically costs less than out-of-network care. Many people pay between $0-$500 per day for inpatient treatment after meeting their deductible, though costs vary significantly by plan.
