Does Medicare Cover Drug & Alcohol Rehab?

Understanding your Medicare benefits for addiction treatment

Medicare Addiction Treatment Coverage Overview

Medicare provides coverage for substance abuse treatment through multiple parts of the program. Over 60 million Americans are enrolled in Medicare, and many don't realize their benefits include comprehensive addiction treatment coverage.

Important: Medicare covers addiction treatment as a medical necessity, not a luxury. You have the same right to treatment for substance use disorders as for any other medical condition.

✓Part A: Inpatient

Hospital-based detox and inpatient psychiatric care for addiction

✓Part B: Outpatient

Therapy, counseling, PHP, IOP, and alcohol screenings

✓Part D: Medications

Prescription coverage for MAT medications at pharmacies

✓Part C: Advantage Plans

All Medicare benefits plus potential additional coverage

Medicare Part A Coverage Details

Medicare Part A (Hospital Insurance) covers:

  • Inpatient hospital care for detoxification
  • Inpatient psychiatric facility stays (up to 190 lifetime days)
  • Skilled nursing facility care following hospital stays
  • Hospital-based rehabilitation programs

Medicare Part B Coverage Details

Medicare Part B (Medical Insurance) covers:

  • Outpatient substance abuse treatment
  • Partial hospitalization programs (PHP)
  • Individual and group therapy
  • Medication-assisted treatment (MAT)
  • Annual alcohol misuse screenings (free, no copay)
  • Brief counseling interventions (up to 4 sessions per year)

How to Use Medicare for Rehab

Understanding how to access your Medicare benefits for addiction treatment:

  1. Verify your Medicare coverage — Check whether you have Original Medicare (Parts A & B) or a Medicare Advantage plan, as this affects your provider options and costs.
  2. Find Medicare-accepting facilities — Use our directory or Medicare.gov to locate treatment centers that accept Medicare assignment.
  3. Understand your costs — Part A has a deductible per benefit period; Part B typically covers 80% after your deductible, leaving 20% coinsurance.
  4. Consider Medigap coverage — Medicare Supplement (Medigap) policies can help cover copays, coinsurance, and deductibles.
  5. Contact the facility — Treatment centers can verify your specific Medicare benefits and explain your out-of-pocket costs.

Frequently Asked Questions About Medicare Rehab Coverage

Does Medicare cover drug and alcohol rehab?

Yes, Medicare provides coverage for substance abuse treatment. Medicare Part A covers inpatient hospital-based treatment, Part B covers outpatient services and some inpatient care at non-hospital facilities, and Medicare Advantage plans often include additional behavioral health benefits.

What types of rehab does Medicare Part A cover?

Medicare Part A (Hospital Insurance) covers inpatient addiction treatment at hospitals and inpatient psychiatric facilities. This includes medical detoxification, inpatient rehabilitation stays, and psychiatric hospital care for substance use disorders. You must meet the Part A deductible.

What addiction services does Medicare Part B cover?

Medicare Part B covers outpatient substance abuse treatment including individual and group therapy, partial hospitalization programs (PHP), intensive outpatient programs (IOP), medication-assisted treatment (MAT), and annual alcohol screenings. Part B typically covers 80% of approved costs after your deductible.

Does Medicare cover medication-assisted treatment (MAT)?

Yes, Medicare Part B covers medication-assisted treatment for opioid use disorder, including medications like buprenorphine (Suboxone), naltrexone (Vivitrol), and methadone when provided through certified opioid treatment programs. Part D may also cover certain medications at pharmacies.

How does Medicare Advantage cover rehab differently?

Medicare Advantage (Part C) plans must cover everything Original Medicare covers, but many offer additional benefits like reduced copays for behavioral health services, expanded provider networks, and coverage for services like transportation to treatment. Check your specific plan for details.

How long will Medicare cover inpatient rehab?

Medicare Part A covers up to 190 lifetime days in inpatient psychiatric facilities. For general hospital inpatient care, coverage is based on medical necessity. Part A covers days 1-60 with a deductible, days 61-90 with daily coinsurance, and 60 lifetime reserve days with higher coinsurance.
