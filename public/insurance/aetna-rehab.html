Does Aetna Cover Drug & Alcohol Rehab?

Comprehensive guide to understanding your Aetna insurance benefits for addiction treatment

Aetna Addiction Treatment Coverage Overview

Aetna is one of the largest health insurers in the United States and provides coverage for substance abuse treatment as part of its behavioral health benefits. Under the Mental Health Parity and Addiction Equity Act, Aetna must cover addiction treatment at the same level as other medical conditions.

Most Aetna plans include coverage for:

✓Detoxification

Medical supervision during withdrawal from drugs or alcohol, typically 3-7 days

✓Inpatient Rehab

24/7 residential treatment programs, usually 28-90 days

✓Outpatient Programs

IOP, PHP, and standard outpatient therapy sessions

✓MAT (Medication-Assisted Treatment)

Suboxone, Vivitrol, and other FDA-approved medications

How to Verify Your Aetna Benefits

Before starting treatment, it's important to understand your specific coverage. Here's how to verify your Aetna rehab benefits:

  1. Locate your member ID card — You'll need your member ID number and group number to verify benefits.
  2. Call Aetna member services — The number is on the back of your card. Ask specifically about "behavioral health" or "substance abuse" benefits.
  3. Ask the right questions — Inquire about deductibles, copays, coinsurance, out-of-pocket maximums, and whether pre-authorization is required.
  4. Contact treatment facilities — Most rehab centers will verify your insurance for free and explain your coverage in detail.
  5. Get everything in writing — Request a benefits summary showing your coverage for different levels of care.

Frequently Asked Questions About Aetna Rehab Coverage

Does Aetna cover drug and alcohol rehab?

Yes, Aetna provides coverage for drug and alcohol rehabilitation as part of its behavioral health benefits. Coverage typically includes detoxification, inpatient treatment, outpatient programs, and medication-assisted treatment (MAT). The specific coverage depends on your plan type and network status of the treatment facility.

What types of rehab does Aetna cover?

Aetna covers multiple levels of addiction treatment including medical detoxification, residential/inpatient rehabilitation, partial hospitalization programs (PHP), intensive outpatient programs (IOP), standard outpatient therapy, and medication-assisted treatment for opioid and alcohol use disorders.

How do I verify my Aetna rehab benefits?

You can verify your Aetna benefits by calling the member services number on your insurance card, logging into your Aetna member portal online, or contacting a treatment facility directly—most will verify your benefits for free. Ask about deductibles, copays, out-of-pocket maximums, and whether pre-authorization is required.

Does Aetna require pre-authorization for rehab?

Many Aetna plans require pre-authorization (prior approval) for inpatient rehab and some outpatient programs. The treatment facility typically handles this process, but it's important to confirm authorization before starting treatment to avoid unexpected costs.

Will Aetna cover out-of-network rehab facilities?

Aetna may provide partial coverage for out-of-network facilities, but you'll typically pay higher out-of-pocket costs including larger deductibles and coinsurance. In-network facilities offer the best coverage and lowest costs. Some plans are HMO-based and may not cover out-of-network care at all.

How long will Aetna cover rehab treatment?

Coverage duration depends on medical necessity as determined by Aetna's utilization review process. Most plans cover 28-30 days of inpatient treatment initially, with extensions possible if medically necessary. Outpatient treatment is often covered for longer periods based on clinical need.
