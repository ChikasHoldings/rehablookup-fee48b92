About Fentanyl Rehab

Fentanyl-specific tracks address the drug's extreme potency and tolerance: buprenorphine induction often requires microdosing or extended-release formulations to avoid precipitated withdrawal. Programs should integrate naloxone training for the patient's support network and screen for adulterants (xylazine).
