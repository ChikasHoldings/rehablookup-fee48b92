# SEO Meta Warnings Optimization Report

**Author:** Manus AI
**Date:** May 8, 2026

This report analyzes the 245 warnings generated by the `check-seo-meta.mjs` script across the 31,918 pre-rendered pages of RehabLookup. While these are warnings (not errors) and do not block deployment, addressing them will improve search engine click-through rates (CTR) by preventing truncation in Google Search Engine Results Pages (SERPs).

## Executive Summary

The 245 warnings break down into three distinct categories:
1. **Description Length Warnings (83 pages):** Meta descriptions exceeding the 165-character limit.
2. **Title Length Warnings (160 pages):** Title tags exceeding the 65-character limit.
3. **OG:URL Mismatches (2 pages):** Open Graph URLs disagreeing with the canonical URL.

The vast majority of these warnings are caused by programmatic templates where long city or state names push the total character count slightly over the recommended limits.

---

## 1. Description Length Warnings (83 Pages)

The SEO validator flags any meta description longer than 165 characters. We identified 83 pages triggering this warning, ranging from 166 to 196 characters.

### Root Cause Analysis by Category

| Category | Pages Affected | Avg. Length | Example File |
|---|---|---|---|
| **List Your Facility** | 48 | 174 chars | `list-your-facility-in-albuquerque-new-mexico.html` |
| **Best Rehab Centers** | 24 | 168 chars | `best-rehab-centers-in-arkansas.html` |
| **Editorial Guides** | 9 | 187 chars | `understanding-rehab-levels-of-care.html` |
| **Canonical Redirects** | 1 | 166 chars | `alcohol-rehab.html` |
| **News Hub** | 1 | 169 chars | `news.html` |

### Optimization Strategies

**Strategy A: Optimize the "List Your Facility" Template**
The current template is:
> "List your addiction treatment facility in {City} {State} on RehabLookup. Reach patients searching for rehab centers in your area. Free and paid listing options available."

*Recommendation:* Remove the state name or condense the value proposition.
*Optimized Template:* "List your addiction treatment facility in {City} on RehabLookup. Reach patients searching for local rehab centers. Free and paid options available." (Saves ~15-20 chars)

**Strategy B: Optimize the "Best Rehab Centers" Template**
The current template is:
> "Discover the top-rated rehab centers in {State}. Compare accredited addiction treatment facilities, read reviews, and find the right program for your recovery needs."

*Recommendation:* Condense the phrasing while keeping primary keywords.
*Optimized Template:* "Discover top-rated rehab centers in {State}. Compare accredited addiction treatment facilities, read reviews, and find the right recovery program." (Saves ~10 chars)

**Strategy C: Rewrite Editorial Guide Descriptions**
The 9 editorial guides use a rigid template:
> "{Title}: Expert addiction treatment information and resources from RehabLookup. Find accredited rehab centers, compare programs, and start your recovery journey today."

*Recommendation:* These should be manually rewritten to reflect the actual content of the guide rather than using a generic boilerplate. For example, the `understanding-rehab-levels-of-care.html` description should explain that the guide covers detox, inpatient, PHP, and IOP differences.

## 2. Title Length Warnings (160 Pages)

The SEO validator flags any title tag longer than 65 characters. We identified 160 pages triggering this warning, ranging from 66 to 86 characters.

### Root Cause Analysis by Category

| Category | Pages Affected | Avg. Length | Example Title |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dual Diagnosis (City)** | 50 | 71 chars | "Dual Diagnosis Treatment in Albuquerque, NM — Find Programs \| RehabLookup" |
| **List Your Facility** | 48 | 79 chars | "List Your Rehab Facility in Albuquerque New Mexico — RehabLookup Provider Directory" |
| **One-off Content Pages** | 28 | 69 chars | "Adderall Addiction Treatment — Stimulant Abuse Recovery \| RehabLookup" |
| **Faith-Based (City)** | 13 | 67 chars | "Faith-Based Rehab in Indianapolis, IN — Find Programs \| RehabLookup" |
| **Other City Templates** | 21 | 68 chars | "Outpatient Rehab in Jacksonville, FL — Find Programs \| RehabLookup" |

### Optimization Strategies

**Strategy A: Shorten the Brand Suffix**
Currently, most templates use ` — Find Programs | RehabLookup` (30 characters). 
*Recommendation:* Change the suffix to ` | RehabLookup` (14 characters) for pages where the city name is long.
*Example:* "Dual Diagnosis Treatment in Albuquerque, NM | RehabLookup" (57 chars)

**Strategy B: Optimize the "List Your Facility" Title**
Currently: "List Your Rehab Facility in {City} {State} — RehabLookup Provider Directory"
*Recommendation:* Drop the state name and shorten the suffix.
*Example:* "List Your Rehab Facility in {City} | RehabLookup" (Saves ~30 chars)

**Strategy C: Review One-off Content Titles**
The 28 one-off pages are mostly 1-5 characters over the limit. 
*Recommendation:* Manually review and trim unnecessary filler words.
*Example:* Change "Adderall Addiction Treatment — Stimulant Abuse Recovery | RehabLookup" (69 chars) to "Adderall Addiction Treatment & Recovery | RehabLookup" (53 chars).

## 3. OG:URL Mismatches (2 Pages)

Two pages have an Open Graph URL (`og:url`) that does not match their canonical URL:
1. `alcohol-rehab.html`: og:url points to `/alcohol-rehab`, but canonical points to `/alcohol-rehab-centers`
2. `drug-rehab.html`: og:url points to `/drug-rehab`, but canonical points to `/drug-rehab-centers`

### Optimization Strategy
These are intentional canonical redirects (aliases). The `og:url` should always match the canonical URL to consolidate social sharing signals.
*Recommendation:* Update the generation script for these two alias pages so that the `og:url` tag outputs the destination canonical URL (`/alcohol-rehab-centers` and `/drug-rehab-centers`) rather than the alias URL.

---

## Conclusion

The 245 warnings are primarily caused by rigid programmatic templates interacting with long city or state names. By implementing dynamic length checking in the generation scripts (e.g., using a shorter suffix if the base title exceeds 50 characters) and condensing the boilerplate text in meta descriptions, RehabLookup can eliminate these warnings and ensure optimal display in Google search results.
